# Section 1: Scope of Cover & Sum Insured

## 1.1 Basic Sum Insured
Section 1.1 — Basic Sum Insured: The maximum aggregate liability of the Company for all claims arising during the Policy Period shall be INR 5,00,000 (Five Lakhs Only) as specified in the Policy Schedule.

The Sum Insured represents the maximum ceiling available across all admissible inpatient claims registered during any single policy year. Cumulative bonus accrued, if any, shall be applied over and above this baseline amount in accordance with Section 1.4.

## 2.3 Voluntary Deductible
Section 2.3 — Voluntary Deductible: No voluntary deductible has been opted under this policy schedule.

# Section 3: Inpatient Hospitalization & Room Rent Terms

## 3.1 Admissible Inpatient Charges
Hospitalization expenses covered include room boarding, nursing, medical practitioner fees, intensive care unit charges, surgical appliances, and consumables.

## 3.2 Room, Boarding and Associated Charges
Section 3.2 — Room, Boarding and Nursing Expenses: Daily room rent, boarding and nursing expenses incurred during hospitalization are capped up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day. If the Insured Person occupies a room with rent higher than the limit stated herein, the Insured shall bear a proportionate share of all associated medical and surgical expenses.

### Proportionate Cost Formula:
Whenever the room category chosen exceeds the eligible daily cap:
$$\text{Room Factor} = \min\left(1, \frac{\text{Eligible Room Rent Limit}}{\text{Actual Hospital Room Rate}}\right)$$

This factor shall be applied strictly to all proportionate costs, including surgeon fees, anaesthetist charges, operation theatre costs, and consultation fees. Fixed medical hardware, approved implants, and non-proportionate medical devices remain payable at actual cost subject to sum insured limits.

# Section 4: Waiting Periods

## 4.1 Pre-Existing Diseases
Section 4.1 — Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period: A waiting period of 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage shall apply to all pre-existing medical conditions disclosed at inception.

## 4.2 Specified Ailments
Section 4.2 — Specific Illness Waiting Period: A 24-month waiting period applies for benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia, hydrocele, knee and hip joint replacements.

# Section 5: Co-Payment Clause

## 4.7 Mandatory Co-Payment
Section 4.7 — Co-payment Clause: A compulsory co-payment of 10% (ten percent) shall apply on every admissible claim amount settled under this policy. The Company's liability shall be restricted to the balance 90% of the admissible claim amount.

The co-payment shall be deducted after applying room rent factor adjustments, non-proportionate cost additions, and any applicable deductible amounts.

# Section 6: Specific Procedure Sub-Limits

## 6.1 Appendectomy
Section 6.1 — Specified Procedure Sub-limits: The Company's maximum liability for Appendectomy (including Laparoscopic and Open techniques) shall not exceed INR 40,000 per hospitalization, irrespective of the Sum Insured chosen.

## 6.2 Cataract Surgery
Section 6.2 — Cataract Sub-limit: Liability for Cataract treatment is limited to INR 35,000 per eye per policy year.

## 6.3 Hernia Repair
Section 6.3 — Hernia Treatment: Coverage for Inguinal or Ventral Hernia is limited to INR 50,000 per claim.

# Section 7: Exclusions

## 7.1 Cosmetic and Aesthetic Treatments
Section 7.1 — Permanent Exclusions: Expenses incurred on cosmetic or aesthetic treatments, plastic surgery unless reconstructive following an accident, are excluded.

## 7.4 Unproven Treatments
Section 7.4 — Unproven Treatments: Any medical procedure or drug therapy not recognized by the Indian Medical Council is excluded.

# Section 8: Grievance Redressal and Claims Process

Claims must be submitted with original discharge summary, itemized hospital bill, doctor prescriptions, and investigation reports within 15 days of discharge. In case of grievance, contact the IRDAI Grievance Call Centre or the Insurance Ombudsman.
